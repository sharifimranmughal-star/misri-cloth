Orders and pricing

Prices are shown in Pakistani rupees. Review the selected fabric, quantity, measurements, stitching options, add-ons and delivery charge before placing an order. An order reference confirms receipt of your request; availability and production arrangements remain subject to confirmation by the shop. We will contact you about any issue or proposed change before proceeding.

Custom tailoring

Please supply accurate measurements and check your chosen garment options. Contact us immediately about corrections. Changes may not be possible once cutting or stitching begins. Product colours can appear different on different screens; ask us about a fabric before ordering if colour matching is essential.

Payments, delivery and returns

Follow the payment instructions confirmed by the shop. Please read our Shipping Information and Returns & Exchanges pages. Returns are available only for fabric, with requests submitted within 10 days of receiving the order. No returns are offered on stitched items, including ready-made garments, custom-stitched garments and fabric supplied with stitching. Return requests are reviewed individually; statutory rights are not excluded.

Privacy and website use

Our Privacy Policy explains how order and support details are handled. Use accurate information and only look up orders you are authorised to access. Do not misuse the forms or attempt to access another customer’s information.
