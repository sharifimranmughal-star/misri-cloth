Size Guide

Measurement Section

Provide these measurements when ordering suits, shalwar kameez, coats, or waistcoats. Visit our shop for professional fitting by our expert tailors.

Suit Measurements

Required measurements for 2-piece and 3-piece suits

ChestAround fullest part
WaistNatural waistline
HipsAround fullest part
ShoulderSeam to seam
Sleeve LengthShoulder to wrist
Jacket LengthBase of neck to desired length
Trouser WaistWhere belt sits
InseamCrotch to ankle
OutseamWaist to ankle
NeckAround base of neck

For 3-piece suits, waistcoat measurements (chest, waist, length) are also required.

Shalwar Kameez Measurements

Required measurements for shalwar kameez and kurta sets

ChestAround fullest part
WaistNatural waistline
HipsAround fullest part
ShoulderSeam to seam
Sleeve LengthShoulder to wrist
Kameez LengthShoulder to desired length
ArmholeAround armpit
Shalwar WaistElastic area
Shalwar LengthWaist to ankle
ThighAround fullest part

Specify collar style (round, band, sherwani) and cuff style when placing your order.

Coat Measurements

Required measurements for overcoats and blazer coats

ChestAround fullest part
WaistNatural waistline
HipsAround fullest part
ShoulderSeam to seam
Sleeve LengthShoulder to wrist
Coat LengthBase of neck to desired length
Back WidthAcross shoulder blades
ArmholeAround armpit

Winter overcoats are typically 2–4 inches longer than blazer coats. Mention your preference.

Waistcoat Measurements

Required measurements for waistcoats and vests

ChestAround fullest part
WaistNatural waistline
ShoulderSeam to seam
Waistcoat LengthShoulder to belt line
Back LengthNeck base to waist
ArmholeAround armpit

Waistcoats should fit snugly. If wearing under a suit, provide suit chest measurement for reference.

Visit our shop for a professional fitting, or call us to schedule a home measurement visit.
