Who We Are

A Family Tradition of Fine Tailoring

MISRI CLOTH is a men's tailoring house built on decades of craftsmanship by the Sabir family. The name "Misri" reflects our commitment to quality fabrics and precise stitching that has served gentlemen across Pakistan for generations.

From premium wash & wear and wool blend fabrics to fully custom suits, shalwar kameez, coats, and waistcoats — every garment is tailored with care. We take your measurements in-store and ensure a perfect fit for weddings, business, and everyday wear.

Led by Imran Sabir, Rizwan Sabir, and Haji Mohammad Sabir, MISRI CLOTH continues the family legacy of honest pricing in rupees, expert tailoring, and customer trust.
