Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, order details and any email address or notes you provide. Custom tailoring orders can include body measurements, garment choices and stitching preferences. Contact and return forms collect the details you submit, including your email, message and order reference.

How we use it

We use these details to prepare and deliver orders, provide tailoring, check order status, answer questions and handle return requests. Order records also support stock management, accounting and dispute handling.

Service providers

Necessary details may be shared with delivery partners and the providers operating our hosting, database and email services. Order and contact notifications may be processed by FormSubmit. Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from a CDN; these services receive connection information such as your IP address. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Browser storage and payments

This website uses local storage on your device to remember your shopping cart and display theme. Clearing browser storage removes those saved preferences. Checkout records your selected payment method and payment status. Do not send card numbers, passwords or banking PINs through our forms.

Keeping and managing information

Order and support records are kept to fulfil purchases and manage related business records. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information. We may need to verify your identity and retain records needed for outstanding orders, disputes or applicable recordkeeping requirements. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
